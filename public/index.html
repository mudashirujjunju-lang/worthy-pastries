    FAQ

    Do you deliver? How much?

    Yes! Delivery is free within Makindye. Other Kampala areas have a small fee shown at checkout. Same-day delivery is available in Makindye for simple cakes ordered early.

    How much notice do you need?

    At least 24 hours for birthday cakes and cupcakes, 3–5 days for themed cakes, and 1–2 weeks for wedding and introduction cakes.

    How do I pay?

    MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money or cash on delivery. For Mobile Money, we'll send payment details on WhatsApp after confirming your order.

    How do I track my order?

    Use the Track order page with your order number and phone number.

    Can I change or cancel my order?

    WhatsApp Norah as early as possible — changes are usually fine before baking starts.
